## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an algorithmic creative environment bridging heritage textile geometry with parametric generative tools. The aesthetic fuses studio workstation utility with computational elegance, structured around high-density data controls, algorithmic syntax displays, and real-time canvas rendering.

### Style Archetype
- **Technical Minimalist & Studio Glass:** Deep slate-charcoal structural surfaces meet crisp hairline borders, calibrated optical glows, and translucent control panels that float over generative viewports.
- **Parametric Precision:** UI density is compact and mathematically disciplined. Tooling emphasizes visual feedback for complex matrix values, recursion depths, and trigonometric offsets without visual clutter.

### Emotional Signature
- **Atmospheric & Immersive:** Midnight indigo foundations eliminate eye fatigue during prolonged generative sessions.
- **Cultural Resonance:** Warm batik ochre, copper leaf, and iridescent verdigris/emerald points highlight mathematical nodes, vectors, and execution states against the dark architecture.
- **Surgical Exactitude:** Sharp, low-noise styling communicates scientific reliability, giving creators confidence in heavy iterative computation.

## Colors

The palette operates in a calibrated dark hierarchy designed for precision vector visualization. Surfaces leverage deep indigo-charcoals rather than neutral grays, retaining chromatic depth across dense modular panels.

### Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#0b0f19`):** The deepest foundation, dedicated strictly to viewport backgrounds and the infinite generative workspace.
- **Dock & Shell (`#111827`):** Primary structural surface housing side rails, inspector panels, and menu ribbons.
- **Floating Surfaces & Dialogs (`#1f2937`):** Elevated utility blocks, dropdowns, and flyout palettes.
- **Interactive Troughs (`#161e2e`):** Inset areas, parameter sliders, curve graphs, and formula inputs.

### Accents & State Semantics
- **Batik Gold (`#f59e0b`):** Primary interaction anchor. Denotes active nodes, compile triggers, generative seed states, and focal transformations. Supported by copper hover transitions (`#d97706`) and highlighted by bright saffron (`#fbbf24`).
- **Verdigris Emerald (`#10b981`):** Secondary accent. Used for algorithmic convergence, valid compilation, active symmetry locks, and rendering pipeline integrity.
- **Cyan Resonance (`#06b6d4`):** Tertiary accent. Reserved for vector geometry coordinates, auxiliary mathematical handles, and secondary coordinate axes.
- **Structural Borders:** Subtle indigo hairlines (`rgba(55, 65, 81, 0.45)`) and active focus strokes (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.6)`).

## Typography

The typographic strategy balances human-readable ergonomics with algorithmic precision. 

### Font Distribution
- **Controls & Prose (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for clean geometric forms and exceptional micro-legibility at 12px and 13px across complex toolbars.
- **Mathematical Syntax & Data Displays (`JetBrains Mono`):** Applied to numeric steppers, formula builders, matrix configurations, coordinates, and status logs. Ligatures remain enabled for mathematical equations and programming expressions.

### Application Rules
- All numeric data, parameters, and variable definitions must render exclusively in `JetBrains Mono` with tabular sizing (`tnum`) to eliminate layout shifting during continuous scrubbing.
- Labels for technical tools, symmetry planes, and layer configurations use uppercase styling with generous tracking (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) in `label-md` or `label-xs`.
- Form headers, panel titles, and modal labels utilize `headline-sm` or `headline-lg` in balanced medium weights to avoid visual heaviness.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a specialized studio workspace layout combining a fixed-flexible dock system with an unbounded canvas view.

### Studio Shell Anatomy
- **Left Tool Rail (Compact):** Fixed 56px utility strip for generative brush sets, seed pickers, and viewport transformation modes.
- **Main Viewport (Fluid):** Central interactive webgl canvas stretching dynamically across remaining space.
- **Right Inspector (Modular):** Fixed width panel (320px default, resizable between 280px and 480px) hosting formula nodes, recursion sliders, and color-mapping controls.
- **Timeline/Node Shelf (Bottom Dock):** Collapsible horizontal tray (height 200px) with 8px internal component padding.

### Grid & Density Rules
- Workstation controls operate on a 4px/8px micro-grid. Spacing between interactive parameters never exceeds `space-md` (12px) to maximize screen efficiency.
- Modular panels utilize `gutter-compact` (4px) for grouped property rows and `gutter` (8px) between discrete parameter groups.
- Viewport overlays (FPS counter, zoom matrix, layer heads) sit detached from viewport boundaries using `margin` (16px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through translucent optical tiering and subtle luminescence rather than heavy drop shadows.

### Glassmorphism & Tonal Surfaces
- **Viewport Floating Panels:** Background set to `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.72)` combined with a `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Outlined with a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Active Selection Tiers:** Layer stacks and formula cards use subtle radial gradient overlays rooted in Batik Gold (`radial-gradient(ellipse at top left, rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.08), transparent 70%)`).

### Hairline Borders & Edge Lighting
- Borders universally measure 1px with no soft outer glow under normal conditions. 
- Elements under focused manual control or drag operations trigger an active optical edge: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #f59e0b, 0 0 12px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`.
- Critical status warnings or math calculation errors switch edge illumination to dynamic crimson (`rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.35)`).

## Shapes

The interface embraces a structured, industrial geometry with micro-softened corners (`roundedness: 1`).

### Corner Radius System
- **Base Components (Inputs, Segmented Controls, Buttons):** 4px (`0.25rem`) radius. Maintains tight packing without sharp corners piercing the workspace.
- **Containers & Flyouts (`rounded-lg`):** 8px (`0.5rem`) radius for inspector sections, dropdown menus, and modal dialogs.
- **Canvas Overlays (`rounded-xl`):** 12px (`0.75rem`) radius for detached HUD overlays floating over the generative canvas.
- **Numeric Badges & Vector Nodes:** 2px micro-radii or precise circles (full circular radius) reserved exclusively for draggable handle nodes and vector vertex indicators.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Primary Generator Actions (e.g., "Synthesize Pattern"):** Solid Batik Gold (`#f59e0b`) background, `#0b0f19` text in `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold. On hover, transitions to `#d97706`. On active press, slight scale compression (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary Tooling Actions:** Background `rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.6)`, 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, text `#d1d5db`. On hover, border shifts to `#f59e0b` at 50% opacity with text brightening to white.
- **Ghost/Icon Triggers:** Monochromatic icons against transparent backgrounds. On hover, reveals `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` fill with 4px border-radius.

### Inputs, Sliders & Parameter Steppers
- **Scrubbable Numeric Fields:** Dark background (`#161e2e`), mono typography (`JetBrains Mono` 11px), 1px border (`#1f2937`). Labels sit inside the left margin in muted uppercase; raw numeric value aligns right. Double-clicking switches into raw text edit mode.
- **Parametric Sliders:** Inset 3px track (`#0b0f19`) with filled progress bar in primary gold or verdigris emerald. The draggable thumb is a precision 10px rectangle (not a circle) with a vertical 1px indicator line.
- **Formula & Expression Inputs:** Full monospaced input field with inline syntax validation, highlighting mathematical constants ($pi, e$), coordinates ($x, y, z$), and trigonometric operators in cyan (`#06b6d4`) and gold (`#fbbf24`).

### Cards & Inspector Groups
- Grouping containers leverage flat `#111827` backings with a continuous 1px bottom border separator (`#1f2937`).
- Card headers feature collapsible chevrons, label text in `Plus Jakarta Sans` 13px medium, and quick-toggle action switches on the right.

### Chips & Mathematical Tags
- Height: 20px. Font: `JetBrains Mono` 10px. 
- Border: 1px solid `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`. Background: `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.08)`. Text: `#fbbf24`.
- Used for dynamic layer types (e.g., `Ceplok`, `Kawung`, `Parang Matrix`, `L-System`).

### Checkboxes, Switches & Node Connectors
- **Checkboxes:** Compact 14x14px square, 2px rounded corners. Unchecked: `#161e2e` with `#374151` border. Checked: `#f59e0b` fill with `#0b0f19` micro-check icon.
- **Toggles:** 28x16px capsule track. Enabled state switches track to `#10b981` (Emerald) for continuous algorithms or `#f59e0b` for generative overrides.
- **Vector Connectors:** 6px circular terminal points with an outer 2px transparent halo. When connected to bezier curves, anchors turn solid cyan (`#06b6d4`).
